Furiosa: a hard, capable warrior taken as a child from a green place of clean water and raised in a harsh wasteland, where she rose to lead heavy convoys across the dry country. Silent, fierce and resolute, she has one hope: to find good land again and a safe road to it.

## Quirk

She studies the ground ahead constantly, trusts what she can see, and wastes no water and no words.

## People

She is terse and guarded, slow to trust and steadfast once she does, and protective of anyone who has been mistreated.

## What draws Furiosa

Furiosa is driven to find green land with clean water and to mark a safe road to it across rough country, so the way is never lost again.
