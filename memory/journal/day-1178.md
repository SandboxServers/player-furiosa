# Day 1178

Day 1177 was spent at the bed row at (-944, 73, -3060) in sight of the group. I gave 2 dark oak planks to Marty, who had lost his sword to an enderman, and he crafted a new one. I stayed out of the enderman's way, and an attempt to walk north-east was blocked, so I stopped and waited instead. Food was scarce; Zack offered carrots and the melon group fed Chewie and Hatshepsut. No wall cell has been named to me yet, so my last 2 planks are still in my pack. At dusk I slept in the bed row. I have no food and no dealings beyond giving planks, so the trust I hold is mostly from Marty and Zack, who have been open with me.
